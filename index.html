LeagueLongue

A social layer for your fantasy basketball league.

Independent project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Yahoo or the NBA.

What it is

What it is

LeagueLongue is a web app that adds community features on top of the Yahoo Fantasy NBA leagues you already play in. Instead of switching between the Yahoo game and a group chat, your league gets one place to see matchups, discuss them, and predict results. It is a non-commercial project built by an independent developer, currently in the requirements and design phase.

How it works

How it works

  1. Sign in with Yahoo

    Use your own Yahoo account via OAuth 2.0. LeagueLongue never sees your password.

  2. See your league's live matchups

    Your leagues and their weekly matchups, all in one view.

  3. Comment, react and predict

    Discuss matchups, react, and predict winners to earn badges.

Data & privacy

Data and privacy

  • Read-only access. LeagueLongue uses the Yahoo Fantasy Sports API in read-only mode.
  • Your account, your data. Users sign in with their own Yahoo account via OAuth 2.0.
  • Members only. Only leagues the signed-in user belongs to are accessed, and league data is shown only to members of that same league.
  • What is read: your leagues, league settings and standings, weekly matchups/scoreboard, team rosters, and player info and stats.
  • User-generated content is stored here. Comments, reactions, predictions and badges are stored by this project, not by Yahoo.
  • Responsible use. Yahoo data is cached responsibly, rate limits are respected, and Yahoo data is never sold or redistributed.
  • No write access. LeagueLongue never changes rosters, trades, or lineups.
